Payment, cancellation & parking

Please read before you message to book.

Mode of payment

GCash QR code or bank transfer. The QR code and bank details are sent once your dates are set.

Reservation fee

₱1,000 per unit, paid as a reservation fee and security deposit. Your booking is confirmed once it is received.

Cancellations

We do not allow cancellations. We can offer rebooking at least 3 to 5 days before arrival so we have time to offer the unit to other guests.

Last-minute cancellations

Subject to forfeiture of the security deposit.

Emergency rebooking

Allowed with proof of hospitalization or an E.R. receipt showing the patient's name and date of hospitalization.

Rebooking limit

One-time rebooking only.

Parking

Each unit has a partner host for parking. The rate depends on the day: regular day, weekend or holiday. Let us know in advance if you need parking.

Group chat

After your reservation is confirmed, we create a group chat so our staff can follow your pre-arrival concerns and updates until checkout.
